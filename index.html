Virdexis: what to know before you order

What is Virdexis?

Virdexis is a food supplement in capsule form, made for men who want to take better care of their energy, stamina and general vitality as they get older. It is sold in the UK in bottles that each last around 30 days.

It is not a medicine. It does not replace a varied, balanced diet, good sleep or regular exercise. Think of it as one small, simple step you add to a routine that already covers the basics.

Who is it for?

Virdexis is aimed at men over 30 who have noticed they feel less energetic or less driven than a few years ago, and who want a simple daily habit to go with their efforts in the gym, at work and at home.

It is not suitable for anyone under 18. If you take any medication, have a medical condition or are unsure whether a supplement is right for you, speak to your GP or pharmacist first.

Everyday habits that affect how energetic you feel

Before looking at any supplement, it is worth checking the habits that have the biggest effect on how you feel day to day. The quiz asks about these for a reason:

  • Sleep: most adults need 7 to 9 hours. Late screens, alcohol and irregular hours all cut into quality.
  • Movement: strength training two or three times a week and daily walking help most men feel more capable.
  • Food: regular meals with protein, vegetables, fruit and wholegrains, rather than long gaps and quick snacks.
  • Stress: long working hours with no downtime can leave you feeling flat, even when you sleep enough.
  • Alcohol and weight: both are linked to how energetic men report feeling as they age.

A supplement works best as part of that routine, not instead of it.

How do I take Virdexis?

Follow the directions printed on the Virdexis label and do not exceed the recommended daily amount. Taking it at the same time each day, for example with breakfast, makes it easier to stay consistent.

Keep the bottle out of reach of children and store it in a cool, dry place.
